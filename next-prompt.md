- Theme: Dark, premium, authoritative

    - Stark White (#ffffff) for primary headings and high-contrast text
    - Subtle Gold (#d4af37) for primary CTA buttons, hover states, and accents

- Typography: Authoritative serif (Playfair Display) for headers, clean sans-serif (Inter/Geist) for body
- Buttons: Rounded (4px), high-contrast gold or navy
- Cards: Subtle borders, whisper-soft shadows, scale on hover
