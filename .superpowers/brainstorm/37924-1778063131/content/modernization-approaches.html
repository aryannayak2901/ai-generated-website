Modernization Approaches

Three strategies for the full-site redesign

Approach A: Component-Driven Evolution

Strategy: Rebuild the design system first (colors, typography, spacing, component library). Then systematically update each page by composing modern components.

  • ✓ New color system (charcoal + accent teal)
  • ✓ Redesigned component library (buttons, cards, headers, forms)
  • ✓ Updated typography hierarchy
  • ✓ Animations added strategically per page
  • ✓ Consistent, scalable approach

Timeline: 2-3 weeks | Effort: Medium

Best for: Long-term consistency and maintainability. Every page gets the same modern treatment.

Approach B: Hero-Focused Impact

Strategy: Maximize impact on key entry points (homepage, practice areas, team pages) with stunning hero sections and modern animations. Polish existing components, but focus creative energy on high-traffic areas.

  • ✓ Incredible homepages (hero, practice showcase, team intro)
  • ✓ Scroll animations on hero sections
  • ✓ Refined but not rebuilt components
  • ✓ Strong visual hierarchy through gradients/layers
  • ✓ Fast to execute, high impact

Timeline: 1-2 weeks | Effort: Low-Medium

Best for: Quick modernization with maximum first-impression impact. Secondary pages remain functional but simpler.

Approach C: Comprehensive Modern System

Strategy: Full-site redesign with design system foundation + strategic animations across entire experience. Every page gets modern treatment with consistent interaction patterns, scroll animations, and micro-interactions.

  • ✓ Complete design system overhaul
  • ✓ Every page modernized equally
  • ✓ Scroll animations, parallax, transitions throughout
  • ✓ Interactive component behaviors (hover, focus, active states)
  • ✓ Premium brand experience end-to-end

Timeline: 3-4 weeks | Effort: High

Best for: Complete brand transformation. Every touchpoint reflects the modern corporate tech identity.

💡 My Recommendation: Approach C (Comprehensive)

Since brand presence is your top priority and you want the entire site equally modern, a full system approach creates the strongest, most consistent impact. Yes, it's more effort, but it's done right.
