Which design direction should we take for the Generate Studio?

Select the option that best fits the premium feel you want.

A

Option A: Deep Glassmorphism (Recommended)

Rich, immersive experience. The panels have a subtle translucent backdrop-blur (glassmorphism) over a dynamic deep-navy/gold animated aurora. Emphasizes depth and a state-of-the-art "AI" feel while retaining the premium brand colors.

B

Option B: Stark & Minimal Authority

High-contrast, very flat modern design. Solid deep navy background with sharp, clean lines and stark white text. Uses gold strictly for interactions (hovers/CTAs). Prioritizes absolute clarity and a traditional legal authoritative feel.

C

Option C: Focus Mode (Centered)

Moves away from the 3-column layout. The prompt area takes center stage, large and commanding. Settings and history are moved to collapsible sidebars or floating popovers, reducing cognitive load and focusing purely on creation.
