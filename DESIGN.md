# Design System: Chambers of Jeet Bhatt

**Project ID:** 10877443945084382441

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere

A premium, authoritative, and strictly professional atmosphere. Designed to convey trust, high-end legal expertise, and precision. It employs a clean, minimalist layout with generous whitespace that brings focus to the firm's legal authority without feeling cluttered. The aesthetic balances high-contrast dark sections with stark white spaces to create a rhythmic, engaging experience.

## 2. Color Palette & Roles

- **Stark White (#ffffff)** - Used for primary backgrounds and high-contrast text on dark surfaces.
- **Light Slate Surface (#f8fafc)** - Used for subtle depth, card backgrounds, and secondary sections.
- **Deep Navy Primary (#0f1729)** - Used for major section backgrounds, primary branding elements, and primary text colors. Conveys authority and stability.
- **Subtle Gold Accent (#d4af37)** - Used for primary call-to-action buttons, subtle dividers, and hover states to add a touch of premium elegance.
- **Charcoal Text (#1e293b)** - Used for highly legible main body text.
- **Slate Gray Text (#64748b)** - Used for supporting descriptions and secondary information.

## 3. Typography Rules

- **Font Family:** Public Sans (or Geist Sans) for general UI elements, combined with a highly legible Display/Serif font (e.g., Playfair Display) for authoritative headers and firm branding.
- **Headings:** Bold, high-contrast, utilizing the primary Navy or stark White depending on the background. Letter spacing is slightly tight to convey solidity.
- **Body:** Clean sans-serif with comfortable line height for dense legal readability.

## 4. Component Stylings

- **Buttons:** Subtly rounded corners (`rounded` - 4px radius). Primary buttons use the Gold Accent or Deep Navy background with bold, tracking-wide text. Hover states introduce subtle scale or background opacity transitions.
- **Gently rounded corners** (`rounded-md` - 6px or `rounded-lg` - 8px) for cards and inputs.
- **Whisper-soft diffused shadows** (`shadow-sm`, `hover:shadow-xl` on hover).
- **Gold Stroke** on focus for form elements.

## 5. Layout Principles

- **Spacing:** Generous spacing strategy with distinct sections (padding of 64px to 96px).
- **Containers:** Max-width containers (1400px) to maintain optimal reading lengths for legal text.
- **Alignment:** 4-column grids for contact cards, 50/50 split for hero or form sections.

## 6. Specific Component Tokens

*   **Contact Card:** Deep Navy Background with Gold Icon and White Text.
*   **Hero Button:** Gold Solid or Ghost-style White outline.
*   **Inquiry Form:** Minimalist dark fields with sleek transitions.
